## 1. FORMAT & THE 10-MINUTE PROMISE

### 1.1 Chosen Format
- **Format:** Interactive Diagnostic Workbook & Architecture Matrix (Delivered as a live, copyable Google Doc + interactive digital intake tool).
- **Asset Title:** *The 10-Minute Bottleneck Audit: How 7-Figure Founders Reclaim 20+ Hours/Week Without Micromanaging a Reactive VA*
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Instant access upon submission via automated redirect to the `/thank-you` portal and direct transactional email delivery (`Email U-1` / `Email Q-1`).

### 1.2 The One Specific Problem It Solves in Under 10 Minutes
7-figure founders are trapped in "Founder Drag"—wasting 15 to 25 hours every week on low-leverage, administrative, and reactive operational friction ($10–$50/hour tasks like inbox triage, calendar scheduling ping-pong, repetitive team check-ins, and basic reporting). 

The audit solves this in under 10 minutes by:
1. **Auditing Operational Friction:** Running the founder through the **4-Quadrant Task Matrix** (Q1: High Leverage / High Energy to Q4: Low Leverage / Low Energy) to isolate their exact delegable hours.
2. **Quantifying Opportunity Loss:** Calculating the founder's true hourly cost (e.g., $2M ARR business = $985/hour founder rate), proving that performing $20/hour tasks costs their business over $15,000/week in lost enterprise value.
3. **Installing Immediate Decision Latency:** Introducing the **Green / Yellow / Red Decision Architecture**, allowing the founder to immediately categorize their tasks so an autonomous operator can make 90% of daily decisions with zero founder interruptions.
