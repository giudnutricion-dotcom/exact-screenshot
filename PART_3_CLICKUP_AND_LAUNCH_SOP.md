## 1. OBJECTIVE & SCOPE

### 1.1 Objective
To establish a repeatable, institutionalized Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) that allows Pareto Talent to take any new B2B front-end lead magnet from initial market research to live ad traffic in **7 business days or less**, with zero lead leakage and strict brand fidelity.

### 1.2 Scope
This procedure governs:
1. Target audience and competitor diagnostic teardown.
2. Production of analytical, high-perceived-value lead magnets.
3. Rapid deployment of a high-converting landing page with 4-question intake qualification.
4. Bifurcated lead routing (Qualified leads to Calendly booking; Unqualified leads to immediate self-implementation Thank You download page).
5. CRM pipeline setup, tagging, and automated email nurture sequences.
6. AI-assisted multi-angle ad creative production and go-live quality assurance.

## 3. THE 5-PHASE REPEATABLE LAUNCH SOP (SOP-OPS-001)

Follow this chronological checklist for every subsequent lead magnet campaign:

### PHASE 1: RESEARCH, ICP & STRATEGIC POSITIONING (Days 1–2)

- [ ] **Step 1.1: Competitor Teardown**
  - Download and review 3 direct competitor lead magnets.
  - Document their gaps: note whether they use generic checklists, unsegmented forms, or weak low-ticket pitches.
  - Determine our counter-positioning (e.g., Pareto positions against cheap task-based virtual assistants by offering dedicated, bilingual executive operators).
- [ ] **Step 1.2: Verbatim ICP Pain Point Mining**
  - Extract exact words from client onboarding calls, sales transcripts, and testimonials.
  - Map pain points into 3 distinct founder archetypes:
    1. *The Trapped Agency Owner* (overworked on client deliverables).
    2. *The Fast-Scaling CEO* (wasting hours micromanaging junior VAs).
    3. *The Disorganized Visionary* (strong ideas, zero operational execution).
- [ ] **Step 1.3: Apply the MAGIC Naming Formula**
  - **M**agnet: Solves a painful, urgent problem for 7-figure founders.
  - **A**ctionable: Gives them a diagnostic tool, not theoretical essays.
  - **G**oal: Promotes a clear outcome (e.g., reclaim 15–20 hours/week).
  - **I**nstant: Can be finished in under 10 minutes.
  - **C**lear: Unambiguous framework (e.g., Quadrant 1 vs Quadrant 4 Inventory).

---

### PHASE 2: ASSET CREATION & VALUE DELIVERY (Days 2–3)

- [ ] **Step 2.1: Author the Core Asset in Google Docs**
  - Use executive typography, high-contrast formatting, and dark/emerald branding.
  - Structure the document:
    - *Page 1:* Executive Overview & Opportunity Cost Math ($985/hr founder time).
    - *Page 2:* The 4-Quadrant Task Audit Worksheet (editable matrix).
    - *Page 3:* The Black Box Delegation Method & EOD Slack Digest template.
    - *Page 4:* The High-Ticket Backend Bridge (invitation to a 1-on-1 strategy call for qualifying founders).
- [ ] **Step 2.2: Verify Access Permissions**
  - Set Google Doc link sharing to: **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
  - Test opening in an incognito window without being logged in.
  - Ensure the "File → Make a copy" prompt is clearly explained at the top.
- [ ] **Step 2.3: Prepare Delivery Copy**
  - Write Email U-1 / Asset Delivery notification with direct doc link and the 3 immediate execution steps.

---

### PHASE 3: FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE & BIFURCATED ROUTING (Days 3–4)

- [ ] **Step 3.1: Landing Page Build (`LP-MAIN-01`)**
  - Implement luxury dark-mode design system: Deep Obsidian (`#0A0E14`), Emerald glow accents (`#10B981`), Plus Jakarta Sans display font.
  - Include social proof stats (<0.1% acceptance rate, 93% retention, 20+ hours reclaimed, 100% guarantee).
- [ ] **Step 3.2: 4-Question Qualifying Intake Form (`FORM-QUAL-01`)**
  - Question 1: Company ARR (`Under $500K ARR`, `$500K - $1M ARR`, `$1M - $5M+ ARR`).
  - Question 2: Operational team size (`1-4`, `5-20`, `20+`).
  - Question 3: Primary bottleneck (Inbox/calendar, Operations, Fulfillment, Sales).
  - Question 4: Hiring timeline (`Immediately`, `Within 30 days`, `1-3 months`, `Just researching`).
- [ ] **Step 3.3: Configure Bifurcated Logic**
  - **QUALIFIED PATH (`revenue >= $500K ARR` and `timeline !== 'Just researching'`):**
    - Show Strategy Call Reservation banner.
    - Route to Calendly (`https://calendly.com/giudnutricion/30min`) with name and email pre-filled.
    - Tag lead: `status:qualified`, assign pipeline stage `Qualified Leads` ($7,500 opportunity).
  - **UNQUALIFIED PATH (`revenue === 'Under $500K ARR'` or `timeline === 'Just researching'`):**
    - Automatically redirect to dedicated Thank You Page (`/thank-you?name=...&email=...`).
    - Deliver instant Google Doc access without pitching a call.
    - Explain transparently why businesses under $500K ARR should self-implement and keep burn low.
    - Tag lead: `status:unqualified`, assign pipeline stage `Unqualified Leads` ($0 opportunity).

---

### PHASE 4: CRM PIPELINE & AUTOMATION DEPLOYMENT (Days 4–5)

- [ ] **Step 4.1: CRM Pipeline Setup**
  - Configure 5 pipeline stages:
    1. `Stage 1: Opted In (New Leads)`
    2. `Stage 2: Qualified Leads` ($7,500 ACV)
    3. `Stage 3: Call Booked`
    4. `Stage 4: Client (Won)`
    5. `Stage 5: Unqualified Leads` ($0 ACV)
- [ ] **Step 4.2: Webhook Trigger & Lead Routing**
  - Connect form submission POST endpoint to CRM / Supabase.
  - Trigger immediate operational slack alert only for qualified leads.
- [ ] **Step 4.3: Deploy Email Sequences**
  - *Qualified Lead Sequence:*
    - Email Q-1 (4 hours post opt-in): Strategy call nudge & bottleneck diagnosis.
    - Email Q-2 (24 hours): Case study: Agency CEO reclaiming 22 hrs/wk.
    - Email Q-3 (48 hours): Final call opening reminder.
  - *Unqualified Sequence:*
    - Email U-1 (Instant): Delivery of Google Doc audit + 3-step action guide.
  - *Booking Sequence:*
    - Confirmation email (Immediate upon Calendly booking).
    - 24-Hour pre-call reminder with Zoom link.
    - 1-Hour pre-call SMS/Email quick reminder.
